Construction company rules, working policies, and client responsibility

This policy explains the basic working rules, conduct standards, and responsibilities of Shri Pawan Shakti Construction for clients, workers, contractors, suppliers, and project partners. The aim is to build trust, safety, and fairness in every construction project.

Important: Every project must have clear written terms for scope, cost, time, payment schedule, material quality, and final handover. This helps avoid confusion and dispute.
  1. All parties must agree on project scope, cost, quality of work, material type, timeline, and payment plan before work starts. Clear terms make business safe and fair.
  2. We work in road, highway, building, civil, electrical, and railway projects. Good quality material, proper supervision, and proper tools are all important for success.
  3. Clients must be updated about work progress, delays, price changes, material issues, and site conditions. Honest communication is a key part of good work.
  4. Any worker, contractor, or partner who joins the project must follow site rules, safety rules, working time, quality standards, and discipline at work.
  5. Leaving a project without proper notice, after agreement, without valid reason, is not acceptable. This causes loss, delay, and legal trouble.
  6. Cheating, false claims, poor quality work, fake records, or hidden cost is not allowed. Such actions damage trust and may lead to legal action.
  7. Safety is the top priority. Workers must use protection gear, follow machine safety rules, keep the site clean, and avoid risky work without proper control.
  8. Payment must be clear and recorded. Any advance, balance, deduction, penalty, or final settlement must be explained in writing so both sides understand the same thing.
  9. Any extra work or changed scope must be approved in writing. Without written approval, extra cost and delay can lead to dispute.
  10. We must follow Indian labour laws, site safety standards, project rules, and business requirements. Illegal work, fake documents, and hidden cash deals are not acceptable.
  11. Every project needs a proper time plan. If work is delayed, the reason must be explained early and a solution must be made. Delay is not a reason to hide facts.
  12. Records of labour, payment, material, machine use, and completion status must be maintained. Good records protect all parties.
  13. Material and work quality must be checked at each step. We do not accept low quality product, fake quality claims, or work done without inspection.
  14. All workers and contractors deserve fair treatment, basic safety, and respect. Abuse, violence, or unfair behaviour is not accepted at any site.
  15. Project handover must include final quality checks, finishing work, cleaning, and proper inspection. Final payment and completion should happen only after quality is checked.
  16. We do not allow theft, fraud, fake invoices, unlawful payment, or dishonest behaviour in any form. This is against company values and the law.
  17. We believe business should be built on trust, truth, respect, and long-term relationship. Shortcuts and fake promises can damage a project and the company.
  18. Clients should also provide timely decision-making, site access, approvals, and correct information. Good teamwork is needed for smooth project delivery.
  19. Planning, design review, site preparation, execution, checking, and final handover must be carried out in a structured way. Each stage matters.
  20. Our company policy supports lawful project work, safe labour practice, clean business dealing, and real quality. We aim to build long-term trust with every stakeholder.
This document is a general policy for fair business practice. It is not a legal opinion. Actual legal matters should be reviewed by a qualified professional. Important Indian laws that relate to such work include: The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996; The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970; The Minimum Wages Act, 1948; The Payment of Wages Act, 1936; The Workmen Compensation Act, 1923; The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947; and the relevant labour, tax, and safety rules of the Government of India.

This policy is made for simple understanding. It is not a full legal document. For full legal review, the law should be checked with a lawyer or compliance expert. Shri Pawan Shakti Construction follows honest work, safe practice, and fair dealing in every project and business relationship.
