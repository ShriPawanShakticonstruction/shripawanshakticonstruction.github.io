Construction company rules, working policies, and client responsibility

This policy explains the working system of Shri Pawan Shakti Construction for clients, workers, contractors, and business partners. The goal is to keep work honest, safe, clear, and professional in every construction project.

Important: In any construction work, scope, amount, material quality, working time, payment schedule, and final completion date must be clear before work begins. Written understanding is necessary to reduce confusion and disputes.
  1. Every project must have a clear agreement on the work to be done, payment plan, material quality standards, working time, and completion date. If something is not written, it becomes difficult to prove later.
  2. Construction work includes road work, highway work, railway support, civil work, building work, and electrical work. Each type of job needs proper planning, skilled labour, quality material, site supervision, and safe working conditions.
  3. Clients must receive clear facts about progress, material issue, delays, site condition, extra work, and costs. Honest communication is the foundation of trust in any project.
  4. Workers and contractors must follow the project schedule, discipline, safety rules, attendance, and work instructions. Professional behaviour is required at every site.
  5. Once a person joins a project, leaving the job without proper notice or valid reason may be treated as a breach of discipline. It can affect the project, team, and site progress.
  6. No party should use cheating, false claims, fake measurement, poor-quality material, or hidden cost. Honest work and proper record keeping are important for long-term trust.
  7. Safety is a serious part of any construction site. Protective gear, machine safety, safe working practice, and proper supervision are necessary for workers and the public.
  8. Payment should be clear and documented. If an advance is paid, its purpose, deduction rules, final settlement, and due date must be known. Every payment should have proper records.
  9. Any extra work, change in scope, cost variation, or delay must be approved in writing. Without approval, future disputes can arise.
  10. Construction work should follow the relevant Indian legal rules and site regulations, including labour safety, wages, tax rules, and construction worker welfare standards.
  11. Project time should be realistic. If delays happen, reasons must be explained clearly to the client or stakeholder and proper solution should be agreed.
  12. Material, labour, machinery, and finance records must be kept properly. Good records help avoid confusion, legal issues, and broken trust.
  13. Any theft, fake invoice, unfair cost claim, low-quality material use, or cheating must be treated as a serious violation. This hurts the project and the company’s reputation.
  14. Each employee, worker, and contractor must follow respectful behaviour, fairness, and professional discipline. A proper site needs order, respect, and team support.
  15. Final checking is necessary before project handover. Site quality, finishing work, safety checks, and completed scope must be verified before closing the project.
  16. We do not support fraud, hidden work, illegal payment, fake approval, or dishonest completion claims. Honest business is the basis of strong relationship and long success.
  17. Workers and contractors deserve safe work conditions, fair payment, clear instructions, and respect. A stable project requires clear cooperation from everyone.
  18. Clients should also respect the project rules, payment schedule, and site safety measures. Good cooperation helps both sides complete the work efficiently and correctly.
  19. Project management must always include planning, measurement, execution, quality check, and handover. This gives a practical and professional result for the client.
  20. We work under the principle that a reliable construction company should be honest, disciplined, law-aware, and protective of worker rights and client trust.
Relevant Indian legal framework often connected with construction work includes: The Companies Act, 2013; The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970; The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996; The Minimum Wages Act, 1948; The Payment of Wages Act, 1936; The Factories Act, 1948; The Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952; The Workmen Compensation Act, 1923; The GST Act, 2017; and applicable labour, safety, and tax rules under Indian law.

This page is a general operating and ethical policy for business clarity. It is not a legal opinion. For final legal or contract issues, proper legal advice should be taken before signing any work agreement.
