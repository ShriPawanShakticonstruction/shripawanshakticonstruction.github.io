Construction company rules, working policies, and client responsibility

Ye policy Shri Pawan Shakti Construction ki working system aur customer, worker, contractor, aur business partners ke liye standard responsibilities ko explain karta hai. Is policy ka matlab hai ki construction work me honesty, safety, transparency, aur professional behavior sabse important hai.

Important: Construction work me paisa, material, labour, time aur project quality ke liye clear agreement hona bahut zaroori hai. Jitni bhi work start hoti hai, usme clear talk, written understanding, aur proper documentation hona chahiye.
  1. Sabse pahle project ka scope, amount, material quality, work duration, payment schedule, aur completion date clear hona chahiye. Agar writing me bata diya gaya hai, to future me confusion kam hota hai.
  2. Construction company ka kaam road, highway, railway, building, civil, aur electrical work ke liye hota hai. Isliye material quality, labour skill, supervision, aur machine use par hamesha focus rakha jata hai.
  3. Client ko project ke progress, site condition, delay, material issue, aur extra cost ki clear information deni hoti hai. Dishonesty ya hidden charge se project me tension hota hai.
  4. Jab koi company ya worker bhi job par join hota hai, to usko rule ke hisaab se work schedule, duty, safety standards, aur attendance follow karna chahiye.
  5. Job ke liye agreement ke baad kisi bhi employee, worker, ya contractor ko bina proper notice ke kaam chhod dena yeh professional misconduct maana jayega. Rajni, rules, aur responsibility ke bina kaam chhorna company ke liye problem ban sakta hai.
  6. Construction me koi bhi party client, worker, supplier, ya contractor ke saath dhokha, jhuth, ya fake claim nahi kar sakta. Honest work, proper measurement, aur real material quality kaafi zaroori hai.
  7. Company ke liye project safety sabse important hai. Site par protective gear, labour safety, machine safety, aur proper supervision compulsory hai. Missing safety rules se accident ho sakta hai aur legal problem bhi ho sakta hai.
  8. Payment clear hona chahiye. Agar koi demand ya advance diya gaya hai, to uska purpose, deduction, final settlement, aur due date clearly mention hona chahiye. Har payment ka record maintain rakha jata hai.
  9. Jab project work half chala gaya ho aur kisi bhi reason se client ya contractor naye condition lagana chahte hain, to usme written approval lena jaruri hai. Without approval extra work, cost, aur delay ka issue ban sakta hai.
  10. Construction company ka kaam government rules, labour law, site safety, and project quality regulations ke according hona chahiye. Illegal practice, hidden money, fake records, or incomplete work strictly not allowed.
  11. Har project ka time limit fix hona chahiye. Delay agar kisi reason se hota hai, to uske liye proper communication, reason, and solution hona chahiye. Delay ko hidden nahi rakha ja sakta.
  12. Materials, labour, machinery, and finance ka proper record maintain hona chahiye taaki confusion na ho aur future dispute na aaye. Good record means trust.
  13. Company ko kisi bhi worker, contractor, ya vendor se cheating, theft, fake invoices, ya low-quality material use karna nahi chahiye. Such act legal problem aur company image ko kharab kar sakta hai.
  14. Jab koi employee ya contractor job me join hota hai, to usko professional behavior, punctuality, respect, discipline, aur team work follow karna chahiye. Construction site par chaos aur unprofessional attitude accepted nahi hota.
  15. Project ke end me final checking, quality check, finishing, and handover process ka formal process hona chahiye. Final handshake ke baad bhi work quality aur defects ka concern clear rehna chahiye.
  16. Construction work me no cheating, no false promise, no low quality material, no fake documentation, and no hidden payment policy allowed hai. Honest business built on trust aur long-term connection se hi grow hota hai.
  17. Har contractor aur worker ko safe environment, fair wages, proper instructions, and respectful behavior milna chahiye. Aadhi job se chhod dena fair practice nahi hai.
  18. Client ko bhi site policy, safety instructions, payment terms, project timeline, aur required approvals follow karna chahiye. Better cooperation se better output hota hai.
  19. Department wise project management important hai: planning, measurement, execution, checking, final handover. In sab steps me cooperation aur clarity hona bahut zaroori hai.
  20. Construction company ka mission quality, safety, dependability, aur long-term relationship banane ka hota hai. Isliye policy ko serious and professional way me follow kiya jata hai.
Company ka principle yeh hai ki project me aisi behaviour nahi honi chahiye jo dhokha, fraud, fake work, or illegal payment process ko support kare. Agar kisi bhi party ko koi problem ho, to uska direct discussion aur proper resolution hi best method hai.

Yeh page general policy ka example hai. Exact legal interpretation ke liye proper legal consultation avashyak ho sakta hai. Shri Pawan Shakti Construction is committed to fair dealing, safe work culture, and responsible project execution.
